# Customer AWS Cross-Account Access Setup

This guide walks you through creating an AWS IAM role that allows Memento to access resources in your AWS account. Memento uses this role to read log files from your S3 bucket for analysis — it never stores long-lived credentials.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, collect the following from your Memento account team:

- **Memento AWS Account ID** — the 12-digit AWS account ID for Memento's infrastructure
- **Customer ID** — your Memento customer identifier (e.g., `acme001`)
- **External ID** — a project-specific secret in the format `ext-{uuid}`, generated by Memento and unique to your project

**Verify before you trust.** Confirm these values through a channel you already trust — your named account contact, an existing email thread, or your onboarding ticket — not solely from an unsolicited email or a link. If anything about how you received them feels off, confirm with Memento directly before creating a role that grants access to your account.

## Step 1: Create the IAM Role

1. Sign in to the [AWS IAM Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/iam/).
2. In the left navigation, choose **Roles**, then **Create role**.
3. Under **Trusted entity type**, select **Custom trust policy**.
4. Paste the trust policy below, replacing the placeholders with the values from your Memento account team:

```json
{
  "Version": "2012-10-17",
  "Statement": [
    {
      "Effect": "Allow",
      "Principal": {
        "AWS": "arn:aws:iam::MEMENTO_AWS_ACCOUNT_ID:role/worker-CUSTOMER_ID"
      },
      "Action": "sts:AssumeRole",
      "Condition": {
        "StringEquals": {
          "sts:ExternalId": "EXTERNAL_ID_FROM_MEMENTO"
        }
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

| Placeholder | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| `MEMENTO_AWS_ACCOUNT_ID` | Provided by your Memento account team |
| `CUSTOMER_ID` | Your Memento customer identifier, provided by your account team |
| `EXTERNAL_ID_FROM_MEMENTO` | Provided by your Memento account team (format: `ext-{uuid}`) |

5. Click **Next**.

## Step 2: Attach a Permissions Policy

Attach a policy granting Memento the minimum permissions it needs. For S3 log access, create an inline policy with the following JSON, replacing `YOUR_LOG_BUCKET` with your bucket name:

```json
{
  "Version": "2012-10-17",
  "Statement": [
    {
      "Effect": "Allow",
      "Action": [
        "s3:GetObject",
        "s3:ListBucket"
      ],
      "Resource": [
        "arn:aws:s3:::YOUR_LOG_BUCKET",
        "arn:aws:s3:::YOUR_LOG_BUCKET/*"
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

> **Least privilege:** Grant access only to the specific bucket (or prefix) containing logs Memento should analyze. Do not grant `s3:*` or account-wide access.

## Step 3: Name and Create the Role

1. Name the role **`MementoAccess`** — this is the default name Memento expects.
   - If you need to use a different name, coordinate with your Memento account team before proceeding.
2. Optionally add a description, then click **Create role**.
3. After creation, open the role and copy its **ARN** (format: `arn:aws:iam::YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID:role/MementoAccess`).

## Step 4: Provide the ARN to Memento

In the Memento Portal, navigate to **Project Settings → AWS Cross-Account Access** and enter the Role ARN you copied in Step 3.

Memento will immediately validate the role by attempting a test AssumeRole call. If validation fails, check that:
- The trust policy contains the exact Principal ARN provided by your account team
- The External ID in the trust policy matches exactly (it is case-sensitive)
- The role name matches what your account team expects

## Security Notes

**External ID protection:** The external ID prevents confused deputy attacks — a scenario where a malicious third party tricks Memento into assuming your role on their behalf. Memento generates a unique external ID per project; it is shared only with your organization. Do not share this value externally.

**Scoped trust:** The trust policy grants access only to Memento's worker role for your specific customer namespace (`worker-CUSTOMER_ID`), not to Memento's entire AWS account. This means even if another part of Memento's infrastructure is compromised, it cannot assume your role.

**Temporary credentials:** Memento never stores long-lived AWS credentials. Each access uses short-lived credentials (1-hour TTL) obtained via AWS STS. Access is revocable at any time by deleting or modifying the IAM role.

**CloudTrail visibility:** All AssumeRole calls appear in your AWS CloudTrail logs under event name `AssumeRole`, allowing you to audit Memento's access to your account.
